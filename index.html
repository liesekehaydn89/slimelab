🧬 Slime Science Corner

What is slime?

Many slimes are non-Newtonian materials: their behavior changes depending on how force is applied.

Why is it stretchy?

In many slime recipes, long polymer chains interact to create a squishy material that can stretch and flow.

Why does it feel different?

Ingredients and proportions affect a slime's texture, stretch, bounce, and stickiness.

Lab safety first!

Never eat slime. Keep it away from eyes and mouths, wash hands after play, and ask an adult before trying experiments.
