🔬 Slime Science Corner

🧫 What is slime?

Many slimes are non-Newtonian materials: their behavior changes depending on how force is applied.

💧 Why is slime stretchy?

In many slime recipes, long polymer chains interact to create a material that can stretch and flow.

🌈 Why do textures differ?

Ingredients and proportions affect a slime's texture, stretch, bounce, and stickiness.

🧼 Lab safety first!

Never eat slime. Keep it away from eyes and mouths, wash hands after play, and ask an adult before experiments.
